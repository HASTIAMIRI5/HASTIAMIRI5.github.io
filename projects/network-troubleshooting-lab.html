Network Troubleshooting Lab

Back to projects

Overview

A lab environment focused on practical networking diagnosis, connectivity checks, subnetting, DHCP validation, and documentation of root cause analysis.

Focus areas

  • Diagnose DNS resolution failures
  • Investigate IP configuration and connectivity problems
  • Practice subnetting and DHCP troubleshooting
  • Analyze traffic patterns and network behavior
  • Record root cause, validation steps, and corrective actions

Deliverables

  • Network diagrams
  • Test results and packet observations
  • Troubleshooting reports
  • Validated conclusions
